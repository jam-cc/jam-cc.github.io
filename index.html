Research agenda

Building inspection systems that can reason under uncertainty.

My work connects robust perception with multimodal reasoning and active interaction.

A

Industrial anomaly detection

Learning reliable representations from imperfect, noisy, or normal-only industrial data.

B

Multimodal reasoning

Enabling vision-language models to compare evidence, explain defects, and use tools.

C

Embodied inspection

Letting agents acquire new views, test hypotheses, and close the loop between seeing and acting.

ICLR 2025Benchmark

MMAD: The Comprehensive Benchmark for Multimodal Large Language Models in Industrial Anomaly Detection

Evaluates more than ten open- and closed-source MLLMs across industrial anomaly reasoning tasks, then studies what actually improves their performance.

NeurIPS 2022Robust learning

SoftPatch: Unsupervised Anomaly Detection with Noisy Data

Introduces a noise-discriminating coreset strategy for image anomaly detection that remains robust when normal-only training data is contaminated.

Xi Jiang et al.
Preprint 2026Multimodal agent

AD-Copilot: A Vision-Language Assistant for Industrial Anomaly Detection via Visual In-context Comparison

Combines visual in-context comparison with multimodal post-training to improve fine-grained anomaly perception, localization, and explanation.

Code ↗Xi Jiang et al.
Preprint 2026Embodied AI

Embodied-AD: Industrial Anomaly Detection through Robotic View Acquisition

Frames industrial inspection as an active perception problem and introduces RoboIAD-40 for evaluating robotic view acquisition.

Preprint 2026Tool use

AnomalyClaw: A Universal Visual Anomaly Detection Agent via Tool-Grounded Refutation

A training-free visual anomaly agent that pairs a VLM’s initial judgment with a multi-turn, tool-grounded refutation process.
